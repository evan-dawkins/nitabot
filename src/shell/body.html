Every L and Metra train, right now

A live map of CTA ‘L’ and Metra trains around Chicago. Leave it on like a fish tank and watch them go. Zoom out for the suburbs.

  • Move the mapDrag it. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Tap a trainSee where it's going.
  • Tap a stationSee the next arrivals. Tap ☆ to save it.
  • Pick a lineTap one at the bottom to see just it. Switch between L and Metra there too.
  • Pick a soundTap the speaker by the title.
  • Leave it beAfter 2 minutes (20 seconds on a phone) it rides along with live trains. Close the train's card (×) to stop.

Not affiliated with the Chicago Transit Authority (CTA) or Metra. Train positions and times come from CTA's public Train Tracker data and Metra's public GTFS feeds.
