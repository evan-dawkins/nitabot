    Every CTA ‘L’ and Metra train, on one map

    Each arrow is a real train, drawn where it last reported. Leave it running like a fish tank, or dig in.

    1. 1Drag, scroll or pinch to move and zoom. Zoom out for all of Metra.
    2. 2Tap a train for where it’s headed. Follow rides along.
    3. 3Tap a station for its next trains. ☆ Save station keeps it.
    4. 4Alerts (top right) has service notices. The line bar (bottom) shows one line at a time.

    Leave it alone and the buttons fade away, then it rides along with a train. Sound (brown noise and spoken check-ins) starts with your first click or tap; the View menu by the title changes or mutes it, and holds cute trains, the satellite map and dark mode.
