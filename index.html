    Live map

    Every CTA ‘L’ and Metra train, on one map

    Each arrow is a real train, drawn where it last reported. Leave it running like a fish tank, or dig in.

    1. 1Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom. Zoom out for the suburbs and every Metra line.
    2. 2Tap a train to see where it’s headed, then Follow to ride along.
    3. 3Tap a station for its next trains. ☆ pins it to your board.
    4. 4Alerts (top right) has CTA and Metra service notices. The line bar (bottom) shows one line at a time.

    By the title: sound, cute trains, satellite, light or dark. Leave it alone for a couple of minutes and it rides along with a train.

    NITABot is an unofficial personal project made for fun and is not affiliated with NITA. It isn’t affiliated with the CTA or Metra either: train positions and times come from CTA’s public Train Tracker data and Metra’s public GTFS feeds.
