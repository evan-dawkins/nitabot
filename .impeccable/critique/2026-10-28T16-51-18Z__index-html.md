## Overall Impression

Honesty and cartography are unusually good. The trains are the hardest thing to see, and the header crowds out the license-required stamp. Biggest opportunity: make trains the loudest thing on the map and the header the quietest.

## What's Working

1. Honesty designed in: source + age on every card, held jumps labelled, failed alerts never read as "no alerts", two-stage Metra health.
2. Ambient mode staged for a second monitor; sound reports real data; train-of-the-moment ride is the peak.
3. Real cartography: railroad-style Metra, transfer pills, 0 station-label collisions, upright labels during rotated rides.
