## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: the map is authored for this product. Real lakeshore, state lines, Metra's rail stripe, verified-only transfer links, honest data-age footers ("Position from CTA's signals, 8 s ago") and the mood ticker voice could not belong to another product. The chrome around it (white pills, a generic chip bar, a plain zoom box, a row of six icon buttons, a shadowed card) is category-interchangeable: any map app could wear it unchanged. Signature moment: showing one line alone, with the rest of the network ghosting back.

## Overall Impression

The map itself is excellent and honest; the frame around it was built for the 25% (quick info) more than the 75% (calm ambient). Biggest opportunity: make the resting state truly ambient (no empty panel, a wider train-of-the-moment view, one legible "data as of" stamp) and fix the contrast and keyboard gaps.

## What's Working

1. Single-line view: one tap ghosts every other line back and labels the chosen one; the clearest, most "NITABot" moment.
2. Honest data copy: every card says where the position came from and how old it is; lateness in amber, per the source.
3. Both themes hold up; dark mode is a real second-monitor fish tank and the zoomed-out region reads like a printed poster.

## Priority Issues

- [P1] No always-visible "data as of" time. Why: Metra's license requires showing when the data was last updated, and the ambient viewer has no way to tell live from stale across the room. Fix: a small, permanent "Metra 7:14 · CTA 7:14" stamp by the Live pill (desktop) and in the status dot's label (phone). Command: /impeccable harden
- [P1] Line bullets fail contrast. Why: white text on Pink, Blue, Orange, UP-N, SWS, NCS, MD-N, HC is 2.7-4.1:1 at 10-11.5 px, unreadable for low-vision users and anyone at a distance. Fix: pick dark or white text by actual WCAG ratio (choose whichever is >= 4.5:1), and keep bullet text >= 11 px (alerts uses 10.5 px). Command: /impeccable colorize
- [P1] The idle ride loses the network. Why: the ambient feature zooms so tight only one track shows, with a dense card on top, breaking "calm first". Fix: cap ride zoom near 0.35x the Chicago view so neighbouring stations and towns stay visible; show a slim caption ("Train of the moment · UP-N to Ogilvie"), full card on tap. Command: /impeccable quieter
- [P1] Keyboard and screen-reader path. Why: about 430 map stops before reaching controls; opening a station sheet or train card doesn't move focus into it. Fix: skip link to controls (or one tab stop for the map with arrow keys), move focus into #spop/#card on open and back on close, add station search. Command: /impeccable audit
- [P2] Empty "Your stations" panel always open. Why: it occupies the top-right on every visit and in idle, competing with alerts and the calm view. Fix: collapse to the star button until a station is saved; fade it in idle like other tools. Command: /impeccable distill

## Persona Red Flags

Alex (power user): no station search; shortcuts only discoverable by hover; ~430 Tabs to leave the map; the idle ride can move the camera while reading.
Sam (screen reader / low vision): focus not moved into sheets or cards; map is a wall of focusable stations; line bullets 2.7-4.1:1; light-theme town labels about 2.3:1; continuously scrolling italic ticker.
Casey (one-handed phone): header tools are a cramped top row out of thumb reach; #aclose is 20x22 px; no "Live" word or count on phone, just a dot; station sheet doesn't pan to show where the station is.
Ambient viewer (from PRODUCT.md): empty stations panel and auto-opened ride card break the calm; ticker is constant motion; no data-age stamp visible from across the room; ride zoom shows too little.

## Minor Observations

- Station sheet: "Add to your board" outranks the arrivals; arrivals error shows no time or last good data.
- "All lines" both resets and folds the line bar; phone line bar scrolls with no edge hint.
- Ticker text cut mid-word at both fades; "WALK TO (FREE WITH THE SAME FARECARD)" wraps in uppercase.
- Alerts filter shows two rows for a single alert; filter counts at opacity .75 drop below 4.5:1.
- Welcome heading wraps with a short second line; "Due" vs "2 min" differs between phone and desktop.

## Questions to Consider

- If ambient is 75% of the product, what would a resting state with almost no chrome look like?
- Should the train of the moment be told through the map (zoom, caption, voice) rather than the rider's data card?
- Could one "data as of" stamp be the honest signal you see from across the room?
